# Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains multiple analytical pages designed to provide different levels of business analysis, from executive-level performance monitoring to detailed product, customer, profitability, and geographic analysis.

---

# 1. Sales Performance Overview

## Purpose

Provides a high-level overview of overall sales and business performance.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Customers
- Total Orders

## Visual Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend
Shows sales movement over time using the Year-Month dimension.

### Sales by Category
Compares sales performance across:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

### Sales by Segment
Analyzes sales contribution from:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### Sales by Region
Compares sales performance across geographic regions.

## Business Questions

- What is the overall sales performance?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?

---

# 2. Executive Summary

## Purpose

Provides an executive-level view of sales, profitability, growth, customer volume, and geographic performance.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Profit Margin %
- Sales YoY Growth

## Visual Analysis

### Monthly Sales Performance vs Prior Year

Compares current monthly sales against previous-year sales using the `Sales LY` measure.

### Monthly Profit Performance vs Prior Year

Compares monthly profit performance with the previous-year reference period.

### Sales by Segment

Compares revenue generated by Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### Sales by State

Provides geographic visualization of sales performance across states and locations.

## Business Questions

- How is current sales performance compared with the previous year?
- How profitable is the business?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- Where is sales activity concentrated geographically?

# 3. Sales Analysis

## Purpose

Analyzes sales growth, geographic performance, state-level performance, category contribution, and customer-segment performance.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- YoY Growth %
- QoQ Growth %
- MoM Growth %

## Visual Analysis

### Monthly Sales Performance vs Prior Year

Compares monthly sales against the previous-year period.

### Sales by Region

Ranks regions according to total sales.

### Top 10 States by Sales

Identifies the highest-performing states based on sales.

### Sales by Category

Shows the contribution of Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.

### Sales by Segment

Compares sales across customer segments.

## Business Questions

- Is sales performance improving or declining?
- How does sales growth vary across time periods?
- Which regions generate the most sales?
- Which states are the strongest contributors?
- Which categories and segments drive revenue?

# 6. Product Analysis

## Purpose

Analyzes product-level sales, profitability, sales contribution, and product performance.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Sales Contribution %

## Interactive Metric Selection

The `Top Product Metric` selector allows product ranking based on:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit

The `Top Product Rank` DAX measure dynamically changes the ranking based on the selected metric.

## Visual Analysis

### Total Sales and Total Profit by Product

Compares product-level sales and profitability.

### Sales vs. Profit Margin %

Uses a scatter visualization to examine the relationship between product sales and profit margin.

### Total Sales by Product

Uses a treemap-style visualization to highlight products contributing to sales.

## Business Questions

- Which products generate the most sales?
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Which products have strong sales but weak margins?
- How concentrated are sales among top products?

---

# 7. Geographic Analysis

## Purpose

Provides detailed geographic analysis of sales, profit, orders, and city-level performance.

## Interactive Filters

The page provides filters for:

- Year
- Region
- Category

## Visual Analysis

### Total Sales by Region

Ranks regions by total sales.

### Total Profit by Region

Ranks regions according to total profit.

### State Performance

Provides state-level:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders

### City Performance

Provides city-level:

- City
- State
- Region
- Total Sales
- Total Profit

### Total Sales by State

Provides geographic visualization of sales distribution.

## Business Questions

- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- Which regions generate the highest profit?
- Which states are profitable or loss-making?
- Which cities contribute significantly to sales?
- How does geographic performance change when filters are applied?
